DEMO DATA - NOT AN OFFICIAL REGULATION.
Article 1 Scope: This simulated instrument applies to facilities operating stationary emission sources in the Kingdom.
Article 2 Definitions: A stationary source means a fixed stack or chimney. Reference conditions mean dry gas at the documented reference temperature and pressure.
Article 5.1 Emissions: A facility operating a stationary source must ensure the quarterly average NOx concentration does not exceed 100 mg/Nm3 on dry gas at reference conditions.
Article 5.2 Emissions: A facility operating a stationary source must ensure the quarterly average SO2 concentration does not exceed 200 mg/Nm3 on dry gas at reference conditions.
Article 5.3 Emissions: A facility operating a stationary source must ensure the quarterly average PM concentration does not exceed 60 mg/Nm3 on dry gas at reference conditions.
Article 5.10 Publication: A facility publishing ambient air readings must retain written permission for the publication.
Article 7 Marine reporting: A facility performing marine discharge activity must submit an incident report within 30 days.
Article 9 Records: All records shall be retained for audit.
Article 10 Reduced monitoring: The authority may allow reduced monitoring where three consecutive years of qualifying evidence are available.
